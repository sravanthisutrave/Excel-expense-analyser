# Excel-expense-analyser
Monthly Budget Tracker – Sravanthi Edition

This is a simple and effective Excel-based monthly budget tracker designed to help individuals record, monitor, and analyse their expenses.
Ideal for students and working professionals, it promotes better financial awareness and budgeting habits.

Features:

Easy monthly expense entry

Auto-calculated totals

Budget vs actual comparison


How to use:

1. Open the .xlsx file in Excel or Google Sheets.


2. Enter your monthly income and expenses.


3. Check your savings and plan ahead!
